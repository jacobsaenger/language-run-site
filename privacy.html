Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026 · Applies to the Language Run app for iOS and Android

Language Run does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.

What is stored

Your progress — the words you have practiced, your accuracy, your best distances, your streak, and the items and outfits you own — is saved only on your device, using the operating system's standard app storage. It never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it, and you can erase it at any time with "Reset all progress" inside the app's Settings.

What is not collected

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash or usage tracking. The app has no server and makes no internet connections of its own. The only network-capable component is the operating system's own billing service (below), which exists solely to process the optional purchases. The Feedback and Support rows in Settings hand you to your browser or mail app, which then act under their own policies (below).

Purchases

The optional Explorer's Pass — a monthly or annual subscription, or lifetime access as a single purchase — and the gem bundles are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play, including any Apple Pay or Google Pay card you have set up there. The store processes the payment; this app never sees your payment details, name or email — it only learns "the pass is active", "lifetime access is owned" or "this bundle was bought" and updates your on-device progress accordingly. While you hold a subscription the app asks the store, when it opens, whether the plan is still active; the answer is a yes or no and nothing else. Restoring purchases asks the same store about the Pass; gem bundles are spent in the game and cannot be restored. Payments are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Feedback and support

The Feedback row in Settings opens a form in your browser, hosted by Tally. Whatever you type there is sent to Tally and then to the developer; nothing you write is stored by the app, and using the form is entirely optional. The Support row opens your mail app addressed to the developer, with the app version and your learning language at the foot of the message so a reply can be useful; sending is up to you.

Notifications

If you turn on the streak reminder, one notification is scheduled locally on your device by the app itself. Nothing is sent from anywhere — there is no server to send it. Notification permission is asked in-app, and the reminder can be switched off in Settings at any time.

Microphone (Expert mode)

Expert mode asks you to say a phrase out loud. The app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission the first time you play it, and asks the operating system for on-device recognition only: on iOS the phrase is recognized on the phone or not at all; on Android the recognizer is asked to work offline. Nothing you say is stored or sent by this app, and Expert mode is not offered for languages your device cannot recognize offline.

Pronunciation

When pronunciation is switched on, the word being practiced is passed to the text-to-speech engine built into your device — Apple's on iOS, Google's or your chosen engine on Android. That engine is part of your operating system and is governed by your device maker's privacy policy, not this one. Switching "Pronunciation" off stops this entirely.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, so it holds no personal information about children or adults. There are no ads. The only purchases are the Explorer's Pass and the gem bundles above; every purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play, where parental controls such as Ask to Buy and purchase passwords apply, and everything the gems buy can also be earned by playing.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what is stored or how, this page will be updated and the "Effective" date above will change. Because the app collects nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely.
